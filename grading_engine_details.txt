SAM Platform Grading/Rubric Engine Analysis
1. Architecture Summary
The SAM platform uses a client-side grading engine to evaluate students across three specific roles (Presenter,
Technical Reviewer, Feedback Strategist) during sessions. Grading is based on a structured rubric assigning 0
to 3 scale points, which correspond to specific competency levels. Scores are weighted by dimension and
aggregated to calculate both individual and team totals. All grading metrics, custom overrides, and session
data are persisted in the browser's localStorage .

2. Extracted Grading Configuration
Evaluation Roles & Max Marks
Presenter (P): 40 max marks
Technical Reviewer (TR): 30 max marks
Feedback Strategist (FP): 30 max marks

Rubric Dimensions & Weight Percentages
Presenter (P)
Product Quality (30%)
Depth & Authorship (25%)
Solution Demonstration (20%)
Responsiveness (15%)
Improvement (10%)
Technical Reviewer (TR)
Gap Identification (25%)
Questioning (20%)
Evidence-Based Analysis (20%)
Improvement Direction (20%)
Prioritization (15%)
Feedback Strategist (FP)
Actionability (20%)
Product Vision (20%)
Gap Coverage (20%)
Feasibility (20%)
Impact Prioritization (20%)

Competency Levels / Scales
The app maps raw scores (0-3) to behavioral anchors mapped in RUBRIC_ANCHORS and SCALE_LABELS :
0 : Observer (red)
1 : Contributor (orange/amber)
2 : Practitioner (blue) - Default assigned value
3 : Engineer (green)

Scoring Formulas and Calculations

Base calculation per dimension: (raw_score / 3) * (weight / 100) * role_max_marks
Example: Value of 2 on a 20% weight dimension for TR (max 30) = (2/3) * 0.20 * 30 =
4.0 points

Total Student Score: Sum of all dimension weighted scores.
Aggregate Level Calculation ( gradeLevel ): Maps percentage calculations to proficiency labels:
Engineer (>=75%)
Practitioner (>=50%)
Contributor (>=25%)
Observer (<25%)

Constraints & Edge Cases:
Grading UI is locked and unmodifiable unless block.revealed is set to true.
Form validations ensure that dimensions are saved correctly per role phase before the entire
session can be formally _submitted .
Cycles are implicitly tracked based on session indices: Cycle 1 spans sessions 1-6, while Cycle
2 spans sessions 7-12.

4. Important Functions
Rendering & Initialization
renderGradePhases : Renders the UI for inputting scores according to the scheduled members.
rcRenderRole / buildRoleHTML : Renders the customization interface for rubric items.
showRubricReference : Displays a read-only matrix of behavioral anchors for faculty during

grading RUBRIC_ANCHORS .

Grading & Score Calculation
updateStudentTotal : Uses the formula weightedTotal += (v/3)*
(dim.weight/100)*rb.maxMarks to calculate the individual total for one student.
updatePhaseTeamTotal : Computes the aggregate score for the entire role team (sum of all

members' totals).
gradeLevel / dimLevel : Functions mapping percentages or exact scores to proficiency labels.

Customization & Saving
showRubricCustomizer : Opens the UI to add, replace or modify rubric dimensions and weights. It

utilizes an intermediate _rcWorking global object.
rcUpdateDim , rcAddDim , rcRemoveDim : Modifies the working state of the custom rubric.
rcSave , rcSaveAsView : Persists the customized rubric into localStorage

( sam_rubric_override and sam_rubric_views ).

Result Aggregation & Export
showBatchGradeSummary : Aggregates and displays role scores per student across all sessions in a

cycle, merging specific dimensions.
recordCyclePerf : Records metrics continuously for a student's cyclic performance using stored

session arrays and max score accumulation.
showGradeDashboard : Computes and visualizes grading progression, calculating percentages of

graded, submitted, and un-started sessions out of the full batch.
exportGradesCSV : Iterates logic over gradesData items resolving properties (IDs, SAC labels,

weights, scores, percentages) into CSV-downloadable matrices.
